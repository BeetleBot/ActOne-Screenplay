## Summary of Missing Shortcuts (Priority Order)

1. **`Ctrl+Shift+F`** — Find & Replace
2. **`Ctrl+Shift+H`** or **`Ctrl+;`** — Toggle Hide Fountain Markup
3. **`F11`** — Toggle Fullscreen (standard)
4. **`F1`** — Help Guide (standard)
5. **`Ctrl+Shift+1`–`9`** — Quick sidebar tab switching
6. **`Ctrl+W`** — Close tab (alternative to `Alt+Q`, more standard)
7. **`Ctrl+Shift+M`** — Toggle Typewriter Mode
8. **`Ctrl+Shift+B`** — Production Breakdown
9. **`Ctrl+Shift+T`** — Edit Title Page
10. **`Ctrl+Shift+N`** — Renumber Scenes
