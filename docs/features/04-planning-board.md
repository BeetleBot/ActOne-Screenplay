# Planning Board

The Planning Board provides a visual kanban-style view of your screenplay's structure.

## View

`Ctrl+Shift+P` switches between Editor and Planning Board views.

The board organizes scenes into **sections** and **subsections**, displayed as columns. Each scene is a card showing:

- **Scene number** badge
- **Color dot** (based on scene color)
- **Title** (scene heading text)
- **Line count** (number of dialogue + action lines)
- Mini action buttons on hover (Edit, Delete)

## Section Columns

Page-level containers that group scenes by screenplay section (Act One, Act Two, etc.). Each column shows:
- Section title
- Synopsis text (if any)
- Contained scene cards

## Scene Cards

Draggable cards representing individual scenes. Features:
- **Drag & drop** to reorder scenes within and between sections
- **Preview content** — first two lines of synopsis/action shown with ellipsis
- **Hover effects** — slight lift and shadow, reveals inline action buttons
- **Compact layout** — optimized for maximum information density

## Subsections

Groups of related scenes within a section, displayed as nested boxes within the column. Cards can be dragged into and out of subsections.

## Use Cases

- Visually restructure your screenplay
- Move scenes between acts
- Group related scenes into sequences
- Get a bird's-eye view of pacing and structure
